Muhammad Siddiq Al-Minshawi – New Murattal (Verse-by-Verse Audio)

A carefully prepared collection of verse-by-verse Quran audio recited by Sheikh Muhammad Siddiq Al-Minshawi (May Allah have mercy upon him).

This collection contains the complete Holy Quran as 6,236 individual MP3 files, with one audio file for every verse. It has been prepared for developers, educational projects, Quran applications, websites, and anyone who needs high-quality verse-by-verse Quran audio.

Every file has been processed carefully so that the recitation begins immediately, with no unnecessary leading silence, providing fast and responsive playback.


FEATURES

• Complete Holy Quran (114 surahs)
• 6,236 individual verse audio files
• Carefully trimmed so every verse starts immediately without leading silence
• Clean, natural audio with no added echo, reverb, delay, or other artificial effects
• Consistent and predictable file naming throughout the collection

Available in two quality options:

• 128 kbps MP3 (Mono, 44.1 kHz) — approximately 1.58 GB
• 64 kbps MP3 (Mono, 22.05 kHz) — approximately 815 MB


AUDIO QUALITY

Two versions are provided to suit different needs.

The 128 kbps edition provides the highest audio quality and is recommended for most users.

The 64 kbps edition requires roughly half the storage space while maintaining excellent listening quality. Although the bitrate is significantly lower, the difference in audio quality is minimal for most listeners. It is an ideal choice for devices with limited storage or slower internet connections.


FOLDER STRUCTURE

This collection contains two folders:

1. Verses

Contains all 6,236 verse audio files.

Each filename follows this format:

SSSAAA.mp3

Where:

SSS = Surah number (three digits)
AAA = Ayah number (three digits)

Examples:

001001.mp3 ? Surah 1, Ayah 1
002255.mp3 ? Surah 2, Ayah 255
114004.mp3 ? Surah 114, Ayah 4

This naming convention makes the collection easy to integrate into applications, websites, and scripts.

2. Basmala

This folder contains seven recordings of the Basmala:

B1.mp3
B2.mp3
B3.mp3
B4.mp3
B5.mp3
B6.mp3
B7.mp3

Each recording contains only:

"Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem"

These recordings are provided separately so that applications may choose whichever version they prefer or randomly select one at the beginning of each surah.

Important:
The Basmala should NOT be played before Surah At-Tawbah (Surah 9), as that surah begins directly with its first verse and is traditionally recited without the Basmala.

Both the 64 kbps and 128 kbps editions include the same seven Basmala recordings.


ABOUT THE RECITATION

This collection is based on Sheikh Muhammad Siddiq Al-Minshawi's New Murattal recitation.

In 1965, Sheikh Al-Minshawi completed recording a full Murattal Quran for the Holy Quran Radio in Cairo. Although the recordings were officially approved, he was not fully satisfied with parts of his performance. He later requested that portions of the recording be made again, and the revised recordings were officially approved in 1967.

The version commonly known today as the New Murattal combines these revised recordings with the remaining original recordings to form the complete Quran.


ABOUT SHEIKH MUHAMMAD SIDDIQ AL-MINSHAWI

Sheikh Muhammad Siddiq Al-Minshawi was born in 1920 in Egypt and passed away on 20 June 1969 at the age of 49.

He is widely regarded as one of the greatest Quran reciters of the twentieth century. His precise tajweed, beautiful voice, and heartfelt style continue to inspire millions of Muslims around the world.

May Allah have mercy upon him.
